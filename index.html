How to use ShopSmart

Everything you need, in under a minute.
1. Lists live on Home

Home holds every shopping list with live progress. Create one with New Shopping List, then tap its card to open it.

2. Add items smartly

Type a product to see instant matches from your history. Set the quantity with − / +, confirm the auto-detected type, then tap + or Enter. Adding an existing item increases its quantity.

3. Tick items off

Tap an item's circle to tick it — ticked items glide to the bottom. Tick all toggles the whole list, Clear ticked removes finished items, and every delete offers Undo. Sort arranges the list your way.

4. Recommendations

Suggestions are ranked by how often you buy. All opens your complete history for browsing, and + adds any item with one tap.

5. Your active list

The middle tab always follows the list you're working on. Tap the title to rename it. The ⋮ menu handles export, paste-import, the +/− toggle, and deleting the list.

6. History

A complete record of everything added, with counts and sorting. Remove single entries (Undo included), clear all, or restore the starter items any time.

7. Themes and types

The palette button beside search opens six themes that recolor the whole app. Each item type carries its own color across tags and history.

8. Shop mode and layout

Shop mode (top-right button) gives a large-type in-aisle view grouped by type. Chevron buttons collapse any section, and each list remembers its layout. History arrives with 200 everyday items preloaded.
